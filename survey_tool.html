Before You Begin

The goal of this survey is to better understand your perceptions of safety in South Africa.

  • First: you will say whether you are familiar with each province in South Africa.
  • Then: the survey will zoom in and ask you to rate municipalities and wards inside the provinces you know.
  • Two scores for each place: you will rate both knowledge and safety from 0 to 10.
  • Knowledge score: 7 or more means you have lived or worked there, 5 means you know the area moderately well, and 3 or more means you have visited at least once.
  • Safety score: at the municipality and ward levels, rate the safety of the central CBD. A 5 means average safety across South Africa, 7 means quite safe, and 10 means the safest place in South Africa.
  • Maps: tap a province, municipality, or ward name to locate it on the map. The maps will be coloured by reported safety, with green = safer and red = less safe.
  • Sliders: move each slider from 0 to 10. If knowledge is 0, the safety slider stays disabled.
  • Progress: the survey saves on this phone so you can resume after an unexpected close or connection drop.

Select the province on the map or on the list to identify it. For each province, select whether you are familiar with any municipalities within it. Answer Yes if you have visited a municipality within that province.
